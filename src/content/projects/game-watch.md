I like gaming. I like it so much that I amassed too many devices to play on. For the same reason, I often watch Shows of upcoming Games, like the E3 streams, Playstation's "State of Play" or Nintendo Direct, etc. Now, to keep track of games I'm interested in, I kept them in some note taking tool. Later I had to do research online to remember what it was and where or when it would be launched.

This being a tedious task led me to the idea of "GameWatch. A simple - but of course [over-engineered](https://github.com/Agreon/game-watch/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#components) - tool that will keep track of the games You are interested in. It will automatically detect if a game that was previously just available in e.g. Steam Early-Access, will be released on other platforms and notify you about that (Maybe [Dorfromantik](https://www.nintendo.com/store/products/dorfromantik-switch/) would be nicer to play on the Switch?).

If we are going to check the store pages anyway, it is able to notify you about special offers or release date changes as well. I also added support for Critic-Scores, PS-Plus Availability, Linux-Support, and more. Check out the [Github](https://github.com/Agreon/game-watch) for the full list ;)
